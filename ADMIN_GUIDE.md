# Volunteer & Admin User Guide
### SVKM's NMIMS INTER CAMPUS SPORTS CHAMPIONSHIP 2026-27

Welcome to the official administration portal! This guide explains how student coordinators and sports desk volunteers manage fixtures, enter results, award Man of the Match honours, enter stats, and update the website.

---

## 1. Accessing the Portal

1. Navigate to: `https://your-domain.com/admin/login` (or `/admin/login` on localhost).
2. Enter your authorized admin credentials.
3. Upon authentication, you will be taken to the **Dashboard**.

---

## 2. Scheduling a New Match Fixture

1. Go to **Schedule & Results** from the sidebar (or bottom bar on mobile).
2. Click **"+ Add Match"**.
3. Fill in the match details:
   - **Sport**: Select from Cricket, Football, Basketball, Volleyball, Table Tennis, or Lawn Tennis.
   - **Category**: Men or Women.
   - **Round**: League, Group Stage, Quarter-Final, Semi-Final, 3rd Place, or Final.
   - **Teams**: Pick Team A and Team B from the 70 seeded campus teams. (For Table Tennis and Lawn Tennis singles rubbers, you can also specify player names).
   - **Date & Time**: Choose a date between **27th – 31st October 2026** and time in IST.
   - **Venue**: Specify the pitch or court (e.g., "Central Turf Ground", "Court 1", "Indoor TT Hall").
4. Click **"Save Fixture"**. The match immediately shows up under **COMING UP** on the public sport dashboard.

---

## 3. Entering Match Results (After Match Concludes)

1. Go to **Schedule & Results**.
2. Find the match that just concluded and click the **Edit** icon (pencil).
3. Switch to the **Result** tab:
   - Select the **Winning Campus**.
   - Enter the **Result Headline** (e.g., `"NMIMS Mumbai won by 4 runs"` or `"NMIMS Shirpur won 3–1 (25-20, 22-25, 25-18, 25-21)"`).
   - The match status is automatically updated to **Completed**.
4. Click **"Save Fixture"**. The result immediately updates on the public **Results** tab.

---

## 4. Awarding Man of the Match (MOTM)

1. In the same match editor modal, click the **Man of the Match** tab.
2. Enter the player's name and their campus code.
3. Write a performance note (e.g., `"52 (31) & 2/18"` or `"Scored winning brace in 88th min"`).
4. Save the fixture. The MOTM award is published with the gold star badge on the match scorecard!

---

## 5. Entering Player Stats (For Leaderboards & MVPs)

1. Navigate to **Player Stats** from the sidebar.
2. Select the completed match from the dropdown.
3. The spreadsheet grid will load:
   - Type in runs, wickets, overs, goals, assists, points, or games.
   - Tab or Enter moves smoothly between cells.
   - Validation checks format automatically (e.g. valid overs).
4. Click **"Save & Recalculate MVP"**. The official statistical leaderboards immediately update with the aggregated numbers.

---

## 6. Uploading Tournament Photos to Gallery

1. Navigate to **Gallery** from the sidebar.
2. In the upload box:
   - Select the photo from your device.
   - Type an informative caption (e.g., `"Volleyball Championship Point Celebration"`).
   - Select the category tag (Ceremonies, Team Photos, Cricket, Football, etc.).
3. Click **"Upload"**. The photo appears immediately on the public `/gallery` page with full lightbox capabilities.

---

## 7. Editing Emergency Contacts & Notice Ticker

- **Announcements**: Go to **Announcements** to add urgent scroll notices (e.g., weather updates, reporting alerts).
- **Contacts**: Go to **Contacts** to update phone numbers for the Sports Director or committee coordinators.
- **Settings**: Go to **Settings** to update venue directions or weather notices.
